Our Story

Two frames.
One vision.

VIGZORA began with a simple belief: a photograph is only half the story until it's held, framed and lived with. What started as a photography practice grew into a full visual studio — behind the lens with Vignesh, and behind the frame with Jeyapandiyan.

Every wedding, portrait and product shoot we take on is treated as a single, considered piece of work — shot with intention, edited by hand, and finished in frames built to outlast trends.
